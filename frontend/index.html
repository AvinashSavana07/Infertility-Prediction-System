What do the terms mean?

Plain-language guide to the concepts used in this tool.

SHAP — “Why this prediction?”Shows which inputs pushed an individual prediction higher or lower.
DCA — “How useful is the prediction?”Compares the model’s net benefit with treat-all and treat-none strategies.
ThresholdThe probability cut-off above which the model reports higher risk.
ROC-AUCHow well the model separates the two groups; 0.5 is chance, 1.0 is perfect.
Population-levelUses demographic, socioeconomic and reproductive-health survey data (NHANES).
MetabolomicUses chemical compounds (metabolites) measured in blood plasma.
